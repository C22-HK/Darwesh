OUR STORY

Built for a market that moves fast and deserves better information.

Darwesh Group started with a simple frustration: finding a trustworthy listing in Kurdistan meant relying on word of mouth, unanswered calls, and photos that didn't match the property. We set out to fix that — one verified listing, one honest conversation at a time.

Today our platform brings together agents, property owners, and buyers under one roof — with real Firestore-backed listings, live submission tracking, and MAM AI to help people search smarter. Every feature we ship has to answer one question: does this make the process more honest?
